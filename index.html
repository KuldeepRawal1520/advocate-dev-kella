About Dev Kella

Tradition in law.
Perspective for what’s next.

Advocate Dev Sanjay Kella is based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. His professional profile connects legal practice with an interest in the questions emerging where technology, regulation and everyday life meet.

Alongside his association with Kella & Kella Advocates, publicly available professional information identifies him with the Gujarat High Court Advocates Association (GHAA). His stated approach centres on collaborative leadership and making space for the perspectives of younger members of the legal community.

Public engagement has also included speaking on legal and social developments, including a seminar addressing the Uniform Civil Code (UCC).
